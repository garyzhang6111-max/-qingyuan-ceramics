From Clay to Fire

Six steps, three days, a dialogue with fire.

The Clay

Local clay, chosen for the piece's color and character.

Throwing

Hand-formed, bearing the marks of fingers and tools.

Drying

Left to dry in the shade before it can enter the kiln.

Loading

Placed along the fire's path — position foreshadows fate.

The Fire

Three days of feeding wood, temperature and airflow painting inside.

The Opening

Ash settles into glaze — opening the kiln reveals the answer.
