A Chronicle of Creation

2010

The Qingyuan Ceramics Studio was founded in 2010 in Songzhuang, Beijing. Founder Yu Wei studied under Zhang Yongjun, a Chinese ceramic art master of the Cizhou kiln tradition, inheriting the orthodox techniques of the northern kilns and the core of Eastern aesthetics. Over the years he has rooted himself in tradition and inner cultivation, steadily exploring the space between ancient craft and contemporary art, and building a ceramic language all his own.

2010–2015

In its first five years, the studio moved between the great ancient kiln sites of Hebei and Henan, studying the essence of northern traditional ceramics. The Henan kiln centered on ancient wood-fired naked firing, producing classic monochrome-glazed celadon tea ware and display vessels, delving into the material character and classical aesthetics of ancient kiln ceramics and recreating the authentic texture and warm luster of time-honored forms. The Hebei studio focused on Cizhou's signature craft — white slip as the creative core, alongside oil-spot and iron-flower black-glaze kiln transformations — passing on Cizhou's rustic character and kiln-change poetry in every piece.

2016–2020

From 2016 to 2020, with its ancient foundations firmly laid, Qingyuan began to iterate and expand. Drawing on years of accumulated craft, it enriched the forms and expressions of white-slip work and focused on wood-fired naked-fired everyday ware. While preserving the natural texture of wood fire and the warm feel of kiln fire, it refined forms and details, balancing practicality with artistry to bring an ancient craft into contemporary life and daily scenes.

2020 —

Since 2020, Qingyuan has pushed beyond traditional boundaries, stepping outside the confines of glaze-based creation to pursue ceramics that unite contemporary concepts with Eastern philosophy. Yu Wei innovatively took saggar bodies as a core material, combined with mature white-slip techniques, to craft unglazed contemporary ceramic works. Through this new creative system, the studio has produced the "Planet," "Moon Jar," and "Pyramid" — its "Symbiosis" series — and spun off the "Light-Keeper" lamp series built on the aesthetics of light and shadow. Its product line now spans tea ware, coffee ware, display objects and installation art, deeply fusing traditional craft with contemporary aesthetics and modern living.

Origin

Qingyuan has always held to a simple, humble creative intention — using ancient wood-fired naked firing as its vessel to explore the depth of Eastern philosophy. Creation never lets deliberate technique override the material; instead it respects the nature of the clay and follows the path of the flame, restoring, in a selfless unfolding, the truest vitality of ceramics.
